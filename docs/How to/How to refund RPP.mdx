When returning spent roleplay points to a player, **do not** use **Reward RPP**.

## Refunding RPP

Use **Spend RPP** with a **minus sign** before the number.

Example: to refund 5 RPP, enter **`-5`**.

## After Handing Out Signatures

When awarding a signature via **Reward Spell**, also use **Spend RPP** for the noted cost. Then:

1. Teleport to the player and open the debug menu.
2. Go to **spells** and open the newly awarded spell.
3. Set `can_refund` to **0**.
4. If the signature is an aura, also set `can_refund` to **0** on the associated tree's **base aura** — this keeps the aura at 15 total RPP while 10 still counts toward their spell tree for intermediates.
