Below you will find a guide for most beastkin races and others; remember to not adjust them without good reason. Usually we do not remove ears/tails/wings without good justification. Especially for Batkin **Dhampirs**

## Batkin Wings

We do not hide batking wings/ears

To hide Batkin wings, set `remove_tail` to **1**.

## Weird Beastkin Ears and Tails

Requires the player **to self-perm first**.

1. Use their **Hair** item (the one they are actively wearing).
2. Debug the hair item -> **Remove Ears/Tails**.
3. **Character bind** the item.

## Force Cosmic Color

Set a player's `star_color` to one of:

- `"blue"`
- `"crimson"`
- `"rainbow"`
- `"rosegold"`
- `"starlight"`

## If they get stuck (and they will get stuck)

1. Set the **Species** value.
2. Change the player's icon (set it to base male/female dmi or icon of their choice)
3. Have them **relog**.
