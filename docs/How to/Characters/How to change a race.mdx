Every now and then, a player character will have their race changed through IC or OOC means.

1. Change the player into their desired race.
2. Using the **Remove-Spell** button, remove their passives.
3. Refund racials that cost RPP.
4. Ask them to relog and make sure their racials/passives from the past race are gone.

Double check if they still kept their racials from previous race, most common case of this is the Human Passives.
