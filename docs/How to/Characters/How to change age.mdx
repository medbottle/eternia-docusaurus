Sometimes players will need their age adjusted, always do this within reason; them being if they spawned one year early or late, they had medical leave and asked to have their age paused, and amidst others.

1. Open Edit menu.
2. Change their age.
3. If they asked to be aged down or aged up on creation, also change `creation_date` in debug menu.

:::tip
If the character uses **Shapeshift**, their disguise age is stored separately. See [How to update shapeshift](/How%20to/Characters/How%20to%20update%20shapeshift).
:::
