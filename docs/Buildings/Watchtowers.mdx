# Watchtowers

Watchtowers are structures that settlements can request to place on the map, providing mechanical benefits.

## Benefits

- Increase the scouting range of faction members while they are near the watchtower.
- Can modify capture rules when certain conditions are met.

## Placement Rules

- A watchtower must be **within 50 tiles** of the owning settlement for its capture mechanics to function.
  - If it is **more than 50 tiles away**, it **only** grants the increased scouting range.
- Each settlement may have a **maximum of 3 watchtowers**.
- Only **one watchtower may be under construction at a time**.
- To begin construction:
  1. Pay the cost listed in the **Construction** menu.
  2. Place a descriptor at the desired location for **3 days**.
  3. If another player wishes to contest the claim, they must respond to the descriptor.
  4. Open a ticket to begin construction.

## Watchtower Range

A watchtower's active area is determined by its **in-game range (~15 tiles)** or while a character is **inside the watchtower**.

> **Note:** If there is any confusion regarding whether a character is within the watchtower's range, staff may be consulted through a ticket.

The following rules **only apply** if a character is scouted **within** this active range.

They **do not apply** if:

- The character is scouted outside the ~15 tile active range (even if spotted due to the increased scouting radius).
- The guard or watchtower representative is outside the active range.

## Capture Rules

### Capturing Intruders

If a person is scouted **within the active range** of a watchtower, the watchtower's faction may initiate a **Capture 1** battle if they choose.

### Defending the Watchtower

If someone attacks:

- a member of the watchtower's faction, or
- an individual who has been declared an ally by a watchtower faction member present in the scene,

the defending side may impose a **Capture 0** battle to force the attacker away, regardless of whether the defender wins or loses.

## Summary

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Within 50 tiles of settlement | Increased scouting range + capture mechanics |
| Beyond 50 tiles of settlement | Increased scouting range only |
| Maximum watchtowers per settlement | 3 |
| Concurrent construction limit | 1 |
| Construction claim duration | 3 days |
| Active watchtower range | ~15 tiles / inside the tower |
| Intruder scouted within active range | Capture 1 may be imposed |
| Attacker assaults defender or declared ally at watchtower | Capture 0 may be imposed |
