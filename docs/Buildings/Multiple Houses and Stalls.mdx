Characters are restricted to owning **one house lot** and **one stall lot**.

:::warning
Owning more than one house lot or more than one stall lot can result in the **excess being removed** from the character.
:::

## Exceptions

**Businesses** and **guildhouses** may be exceptions to this rule. If you are unsure whether a property counts as either, **open a ticket** and confirm with staff before proceeding.

## Stall upkeep

Shops must be actively maintained:

- If a stall has a **lack of items** and is **not regularly restocked**, it may be **taken from the owner and put up for sale**.
- You **cannot** use a stall as storage by leaving items **without a price**.

## Unpriced items

Players may ticket to try and attempt to adquire an unpriced item; this will be judged on a ticket-by-ticket basis.
