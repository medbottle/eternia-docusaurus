## Spawnables Coords

Staff members may spawn various spawnable items that players have access to by teleporting to the coordinates **1, 1, 3**. Most, if not all, items that players spawn every now and then can be found here.

When spawning/taking an item, don't forget to **DUPLICATE** the item first and take the copy.
