Unique weapons get previous modifiers (master, artisan, grandwork and tags such as paladin) if a weapon is used in the process and/or is an upgrade. (must confirm??)

But they do not get to have ULTRA, RARE, UNCOMMON rarity. However, the added bonus from the rarity is put into the base stats.

If an ultra staff has 30 power base and +5 from the modifier; it should have 35 power in the unique.

Sprite changes are exclusively to UNIQUES; so witchblade/paladin/blessed/profaned/artisan and similar do not benefit of a sprite change.
